## How much of the bonus is actually cash?

Less than the headline. The €100 X-Perps Voucher in the first table is the clearest example: it gives you a €100 position built from €20 of margin at 5x leverage. You keep only the profit that position generates. The €20 margin is not paid to you, and if the position closes at a loss you receive nothing. At the page's stated conversion of 1 EUR = 1.15 USDC, it displays as 115 USDC.

The Full Rebate programme splits rewards into three formats with the same logic:

- **Trading Bonus** — usable for derivatives trading; profits withdrawable, principal not.
- **Contract Voucher** — a one-off perpetual contract position; profits withdrawable, principal not.
- **Rebate Card** — returns trading fees; the rebate is withdrawable, the card principal is not.

The Bitcoin rewards on the EEA page are the exception: those are real BTC, subject to the lock-up described next.

## When the bonus becomes withdrawable

Four different clocks run on OKX rewards, and they belong to different programmes, so do not average them.

- **30 days.** On the €200 partner campaign, the cash equivalent value of rewards becomes withdrawable after 30 days.
- **Up to 45 days.** Crypto rewards under the international new user terms can sit in a qualifying period of up to 45 days, tradeable but not withdrawable.
- **Up to 90 days, with a balance condition.** The international terms restrict rewards from trading or withdrawal for up to 90 days; unlocking requires holding a portfolio balance above a threshold for a cumulative 30 days inside that window, and those 30 days do not need to be consecutive. The EEA page states the same 90-day cap but says the 30 qualifying days must be consecutive. Two different documents, two different rules, so read the terms attached to the campaign you actually joined.
- **30 days to claim.** International terms require you to claim a reward within 30 days of issuance. Daily manual claiming applies to the Full Rebate batches, and unclaimed days expire.

Crediting is not instant either. Task completion and reward distribution can take up to 48 hours, and the international terms allow up to 14 days after conditions are verified.

## Four rules that decide whether you qualify at all

This is where most failed claims come from, and none of it appears in the headline.

### 1. Net deposit is deposits minus withdrawals

OKX calculates Net Deposit as qualifying deposits minus qualifying withdrawals. Internal transfers between OKX accounts do not count as a deposit. An outgoing internal transfer to another OKX user can reduce your net figure. The terms give a worked example: deposit 10,000, withdraw 2,000, and your net deposit is 8,000. Receiving a 1,000 internal transfer from another user does not push it back to 9,000. If your net deposit drops below the required amount before verification or before a holding period ends, the task may no longer be satisfied.

### 2. Stablecoin and zero-fee pairs do not count as trading

Volume requirements specify non-stablecoin pairs, and the exclusion list is broader than most people expect. Trades on 0-fee pairs and stablecoin pairs such as USDT/USD, USDT/USDC, USDT/USDG, PYUSD/USDT and RLUSD/USDT are excluded. So are zero mark-up buy, sell and convert transactions including USDC/USDT, USDC/USDG, USDC/USD and USDG/USD. The EEA version adds the USDC/EUR spot pair to the excluded list.

An account that only swaps stablecoins can therefore show plenty of activity and zero qualifying volume.

### 3. The Hold Amount can lock your entire balance

When you receive a crypto reward, OKX places a hold equal to the reward quantity multiplied by its live market price. That hold keeps tracking the original quantity even after you sell.

The official example: you receive 0.001 BTC when BTC is at $100,000, so the reward is worth $100. You sell it and hold $100 in USD. BTC then rises to $120,000, and the Hold Amount recalculates to $120. Your available balance of $100 is now below the hold, so your whole balance appears locked, including the proceeds of your own sale. OKX states this is expected behaviour, not an error, and releases the hold once the holding requirement is met or the reward is reclaimed.

### 4. Moving money into OKX Pay can void the task

Funds or supported assets transferred to OKX Pay reduce your account balance, and that balance feeds reward eligibility. If you are mid-way through a holding requirement, moving money into OKX Pay can push you under the threshold.

## Who should skip this

If you want free cash without trading, this is the wrong offer. The entry reward is a leveraged position voucher, and the cash-equivalent tiers are tied to trading volume. The top step of the €200 ladder requires €10,000 on deposit plus €100,000 traded in non-stablecoin pairs within 7 days. That is a reasonable target only if you were going to trade that size anyway.

- **KYC covers ID plus proof of address.** The EEA campaigns require both, and sub-accounts are excluded from every version of the programme.

## FAQ

**Do rewards arrive automatically once I finish the tasks?**
No. You claim them in Campaign center > Welcome Rewards, and processing can take up to 48 hours. If nothing shows after 48 hours, contact support through live chat with a screenshot of your completed task status.

**Can I join two new user campaigns to double up?**
No. The terms across these programmes say one new user activity at a time, and only the last campaign you registered for is rewarded. Referral commission is handled under separate referral terms rather than added on top of the new user bonus.

**Why does my balance show as fully locked after I sold my reward?**
That is the Hold Amount mechanism described above. It tracks the market value of the original reward quantity, not the cash you received.

**My trade volume looks high but the task still shows incomplete. What happened?**
Check which pairs you traded. Stablecoin pairs, zero-fee pairs, zero mark-up converts and the USDC/EUR spot pair do not count.

**Do I need to deposit the full amount at once?**
Deposits count toward Net Deposit, so the total matters more than the number of transfers. Internal transfers and promotional credit do not count.

## How to decide

Match the structure to what you were already going to do:

- **You want real Bitcoin for a simple hold** → the EEA €100 structure: €200 purchase, 30-day hold, plus €1,000 of volume within 14 days for the extra €80.
- **You already trade non-stablecoin pairs at size** → the €200 ladder can pay out, but only if you reach the €10,000 deposit and €100,000 volume step inside 7 days.
- **You want a first trade refunded instead** → the Full Rebate programme pays 100% of one qualifying trade in batches, and the first trade must clear the minimum in a single order.
- **You will not trade at all** → take none of these; the value sits behind volume or leverage you would not otherwise use.
